zix WebRTC broadcast

One camera, many watchers. Every tab holds one connection, to zix, and zix forwards the sender's picture and sound to everybody else. Nothing here is decoded on the way through: a packet is opened with the sender's key, renumbered where it has to be, and sealed again under each watcher's key.

Open this page in one tab and press Send my camera, then open it again in as many tabs as you like and press Just watch. A watcher that arrives in the middle of a stream cannot decode it until a fresh keyframe comes, so zix asks the sender for one whenever somebody new is put on a stream.

A sender takes what is forwarded as well as giving it, so the picture below fills in for a sender too, with whatever a second sender is putting in.
